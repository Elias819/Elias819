## 🚧 Featured Project

### ObraFlow — Smart Management for Service Providers

**ObraFlow** is a management platform focused on independent professionals and small service teams.

The project is being developed as both a real software product and part of my Software Engineering academic journey.

### Core features

- Client management
- Service management
- Service quotes and payments
- Operational history
- Work diary
- Financial control
- Employee and team records
- Authentication and private routes
- Operational dashboards
- Automated E2E testing

### Architecture & Development

The project follows a modular organization focused on separation of responsibilities, maintainability and continuous evolution.

**Main stack:**

`React` · `Node.js` · `Express` · `PostgreSQL` · `Docker`
