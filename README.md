## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Software Engineering student at Universidade de Franca — UNIFRAN**, focused on building practical software for real-world problems.

My main project is **ObraFlow**, a SaaS designed for independent service providers and small teams that need better control over clients, services, quotes, payments and daily operations.

I enjoy working across the complete development cycle:

### The Product

ObraFlow brings operational and financial information into a single workflow.

- Client and service management
- Quotes and payments
- Work diary
- Operational notebook
- Service history
- Costs and profit tracking
- Team records
- Daily operational dashboard
- Authentication and private routes
- Automated E2E testing

### Engineering Approach

The project follows a modular structure focused on separation of responsibilities, maintainability and continuous evolution.

<p align="center">
  <code>Controller → Service → Repository → Database</code>
</p>

The goal is not only to make the system work, but to keep it understandable and maintainable as it grows.
